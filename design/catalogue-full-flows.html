How it works
01
Everyone sees the category. Every artist but one also sees the word.
02
In turn, each artist draws one unbroken line in their own colour. Lift your finger and the turn is over.
03
Two passes around the table. Draw well enough to prove you know the word — badly enough that the fake can't guess it.
04
Everyone votes. Ties acquit. If the fake artist is caught, they get one guess at the word.
Scoring
Fake artist survives the vote+2 fake & QM
Caught but names the word+2 fake & QM
Caught and guesses wrong+1 each artist

E · Handoff
What a developer needs that the screens don't say.
Round state machine
One authoritative state object per room; both modes run the same reducer. Local mode keeps it in memory and mirrors to localStorage; online mode keeps it in the Durable Object.
lobbyroster/deck/rounds editable · needs ≥5 players
dealingword drawn, roles assigned, QM set (A4, B1–B3)
drawingturn index 0…2n−1 · one stroke each, two passes (B4–B6)
votingballots sealed until all in · QM abstains (C1–C2)
guessingonly if accused === fakeArtistId · 30s (C3)
revealscore delta applied, drawing archived (C4–C5)
closedafter N rounds → C6
Scoring resolution. survived → fake +2, QM +2. caught & guessed right → fake +2, QM +2. caught & wrong → every real artist +1. Tie in the vote counts as survived. Fake-artist duty rotates through the roster before repeating.
Cloudflare topology
Static app on Cloudflare Pages. One Durable Object per room, keyed by the 4-letter code, holds the authoritative state and the WebSocket set. A Worker route resolves /r/:code to that DO and upgrades the socket. No database needed for play; D1 or KV only if you want persistent archives.
join→ {name, colour} · DO replies with full state + your private card
stroke:point→ batched {x,y}[] at ~30ms, broadcast to others (live nib)
stroke:commit→ full point list; DO validates it's your turn, advances index
stroke:undo→ only inside the 4s grace window, only your own stroke
vote→ {targetId}; tallies stay server-side until complete
guess→ {text}; fuzzy match server-side (never ship the word to the fake)
state← whole-state broadcast on every phase change; clients are dumb
Anti-cheat. The word only ever goes to real artists' sockets. Turn order, undo eligibility and vote sealing are all enforced in the DO — the client never decides. On reconnect the DO replays committed strokes as one snapshot.
Canvas & decks
Canvas. Square, normalised 0–1 coordinates so every screen size agrees. Strokes are pointer paths with a 6px round cap, stored as arrays and re-rendered on resize — never as bitmaps. One stroke per turn: pointerdown starts it, pointerup ends the line and opens the 4s grace window (undo restores the turn, not the previous stroke). A stroke of fewer than 3 points is rejected as a mis-tap.
Decks. Four JSON files — animals, food, movies, objects — plus a shuffled "everything". Words must be nouns drawable in ~14 short lines; no proper nouns outside Movies. Never repeat a word inside one session.
Stroke palette — 12 seats
All twelve read distinctly on #FFFBF0 paper and stay distinguishable for common colour-blindness — no red/green pair sits adjacent in seat order.
Not yet designed. Sound/haptics, a spectator link for non-players, and the shared archive page a "Share the archive" tap produces. Say the word and I'll draw those.
